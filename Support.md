## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Are all puzzles solvable?
Yes! Every single board generated is mathematically guaranteed to be 100% solvable.

### 2. How do I change the game goal or mode?
- Use the mode button in the top bar to switch your winning objective.
- Use the Settings.

### 3. How do I change the language?
Tap the globe/flag icon in the top-left corner of the screen to choose from 21 supported languages. Your choice is saved automatically.

### 4. Does the app collect any personal data?
No. The game works completely offline. Your game progress, high scores, and language preferences are stored locally on your device.
