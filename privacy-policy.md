# Privacy Policy — Pentagon Wars: Tactical Domination

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Pentagon Wars: Tactical Domination** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application distributed via the Apple App Store and other mobile platforms.

---

## 1. Overview and Commitment to Privacy

**Pentagon Wars: Tactical Domination** is an offline, turn-based strategy game. We respect your privacy and have designed the App so that you can play without providing any personal information.

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or device identifiers).
- **No Account Required:** The App does not require user registration, login, or authentication.
- **No Tracking or Behavioral Profiling:** We do not track your activity across other apps or websites, and we do not use Apple Advertising Identifiers (IDFA).

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless gameplay experience, the App stores a minimal amount of non-personal configuration data locally on your device using standard on-device storage (`AsyncStorage`):

- **Language Preference:** Your selected interface language (among the 21 supported languages).
- **Game Settings:** In-game configuration options such as opponent count, territory density, map size, difficulty level, and battle animation speed.

This data resides strictly on your device, is never transmitted to any external server, and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the App.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

- **No Third-Party Analytics:** The App does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs that collect user or device telemetry.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The App does not display third-party advertisements or share data with ad networks.
- **No External Data Sharing:** Because we do not collect any user data, no information is shared with third parties.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

The App does not request or require access to sensitive device permissions such as:
- Camera or Photo Library
- Microphone
- Contacts or Calendar
- Precise or Coarse Location (GPS)
- Bluetooth or Local Network tracking

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted, the App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable international child privacy regulations.

---

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Since all game settings and language preferences are stored exclusively on your local device, you have full control over this data. You can reset or remove all locally stored preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

---

## 7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA / International Users)

Under data protection laws such as the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users have rights to access, delete, or restrict the processing of their personal data. Because **Pentagon Wars: Tactical Domination** does not collect or process any personal data on external servers, we hold no personal records about you to access, export, or delete.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App's features or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

---

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact the developer at:
