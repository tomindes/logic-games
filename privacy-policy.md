# Privacy Policy — Logic Code, Logic War, Logic Lights

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the following mobile applications (collectively referred to as "the Apps", "we", "us", or "our") distributed via the Apple App Store and other mobile platforms:

- **Logic Code**
- **Logic War**
- **Logic Lights**

This document explains how information is handled when you download, install, and play any of the Apps listed above.

---

## 1. Overview and Commitment to Privacy

**Logic Code**, **Logic War**, and **Logic Lights** are standalone logic and strategy mobile games designed to operate offline on your device. We respect your privacy and have built the Apps so that you can enjoy full gameplay without providing any personal information.

- **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, IP address, or persistent device identifiers).
- **No Account or Registration Required:** None of the Apps require you to create an account, sign in, or authenticate to play.
- **No Tracking or Cross-App Profiling:** We do not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not access or use the Apple Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a smooth user experience and preserve your preferences between sessions, the Apps store a minimal amount of non-personal configuration and game state data strictly locally on your device using standard on-device storage (`AsyncStorage`):

- **Language Preferences:** Your chosen interface language (such as English, Ukrainian, or other supported languages).
- **Game Settings & Progress:** Local game configuration options (such as difficulty level, board size, number of opponents, animation speed, sound/haptic toggles, or local level progress).

All locally stored preferences remain exclusively on your device. They are never transmitted to any external server and are permanently deleted if you uninstall the respective App from your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services, Analytics, and Advertising

- **No Third-Party Analytics SDKs:** The Apps do not include third-party analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting frameworks that collect personal or device-level data.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The Apps do not display third-party advertisements and do not share any data with advertising networks or data brokers.
- **No External Data Sharing:** Because we do not collect any user data, no information is ever sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

The Apps do not request or require access to sensitive device permissions or hardware sensors, including:

- Camera or Photo Library
- Microphone
- Contacts, Calendar, or Reminders
- Precise or Coarse Location Services (GPS)
- Health, Motion, Bluetooth, or Local Network tracking

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Our Apps are suitable for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). Because the Apps collect zero personal data, they fully comply with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K), and Apple App Store guidelines.

---

## 6. Data Security and Retention

Because all game preferences and progress are stored solely on your local device and never leave it, you maintain complete control over your data at all times. You may reset or erase all locally saved settings at any time by clearing the App's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the App.

---

## 7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA / CPRA)

Under international privacy laws—including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA)—users have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Because **Logic Code**, **Logic War**, and **Logic Lights** do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers, we do not possess any personal records about you to access, export, modify, or delete.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect updates to **Logic Code**, **Logic War**, or **Logic Lights**, or to comply with changes in legal or Apple App Store requirements. Any revisions will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

---

## 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of **Logic Code**, **Logic War**, or **Logic Lights**, please contact the developer at:
